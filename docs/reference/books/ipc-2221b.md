# IPC-2221B — Generic Standard on Printed Board Design (distilled rules)

Provenance: IPC-2221B, revision B, November 2012 (supersedes IPC-2221A
May 2003). Text cache `.book-cache/ipc-2221b/` (184 pages), sha256
`aab1225c227028819ddda3503519a8b4b117b10e1687ce29835ab975bb2a39ac`
per `.book-cache/manifest.json`. Distilled 2026-07-11. Locators are
"PDF p.N" into the cache; the standard's printed page number is
PDF page minus 12 for the body sections.

PCBSmith audit context: our boards are **Class 2** (dedicated
service), **<= 100 V working voltage**, external conductors
**solder-mask coated (B4)** with **uncoated component lands/leads
(A6)**; mains flyback is the exception (171-250 V DC-peak band).
Producibility levels: A = preferred, B = standard, C = reduced
(PDF p.14, sect. 1.6.3). "shall" in the standard = mandatory;
"should" = recommended (PDF p.14, sect. 1.4).

Section map (from TOC, PDF p.7-10): 1 scope/classes, 3 general +
testability, 4 materials + solder mask + marking, 5 mechanical,
6 electrical (clearance Table 6-1, conductor sizing), 7 thermal,
8 component/assembly, 9 holes/annular ring, 10 circuit features,
11 documentation, 12 QA coupons.

## 1. Electrical clearance — Table 6-1 (THE clearance authority)

WHERE: PDF p.69, sect. 6.3, Table 6-1 "Electrical Conductor Spacing".
Categories (PDF p.68-70, sect. 6.3.1-6.3.7): B1 internal; B2 external
uncoated, sea level-3050 m; B3 external uncoated, >3050 m; B4 external
with permanent polymer coating (solder mask), any elevation; A5
assembly conformal-coated; A6 external component lead/termination,
uncoated; A7 lead/termination conformal-coated.

Minimum spacing, mm (voltage = DC or AC **peak** between conductors):

| Volts | B1 | B2 | B3 | B4 | A5 | A6 | A7 |
|-------|------|------|------|------|------|------|------|
| 0-15 | 0.05 | 0.1 | 0.1 | 0.05 | 0.13 | 0.13 | 0.13 |
| 16-30 | 0.05 | 0.1 | 0.1 | 0.05 | 0.13 | 0.25 | 0.13 |
| 31-50 | 0.1 | 0.64 | 0.64 | 0.13 | 0.13 | 0.4 | 0.13 |
| 51-100 | 0.1 | 0.64 | 1.5 | 0.13 | 0.13 | 0.5 | 0.13 |
| 101-150 | 0.2 | 0.64 | 3.2 | 0.4 | 0.4 | 0.8 | 0.4 |
| 151-170 | 0.2 | 1.25 | 3.2 | 0.4 | 0.4 | 0.8 | 0.4 |
| 171-250 | 0.2 | 1.25 | 6.4 | 0.4 | 0.4 | 0.8 | 0.4 |
| 251-300 | 0.2 | 1.25 | 12.5 | 0.4 | 0.4 | 0.8 | 0.8 |
| 301-500 | 0.25 | 2.5 | 12.5 | 0.8 | 0.8 | 1.5 | 0.8 |
| >500, mm/V | 0.0025 | 0.005 | 0.025 | 0.00305 | 0.00305 | 0.00305 | 0.00305 |

- \>500 V rule: add (per-volt value x excess volts) to the 500 V row
  value; worked example 600 V B1 = 0.25 + 100x0.0025 = 0.50 mm
  (PDF p.69, sect. 6.3).
- Values presume woven fiberglass/epoxy systems (Table 6-1 note 1).
- Applies conductor-to-conductor, pattern-to-pattern, and conductive
  markings/hardware-to-conductor; also z-axis layer-to-layer
  (PDF p.68, sect. 6.3). PTH-to-internal-conductor spacing = same as
  internal conductor spacing (PDF p.117, sect. 10.1.2).
- B4 caveat: valid only if ALL conductors except solder lands are
  fully coated; lands/leads themselves fall under A6 (PDF p.70,
  sect. 6.3.4). Not for harsh/humid/contaminated environments.
- WHY (stated): uncoated external spacing guards against leakage from
  condensed moisture/humidity; do not rely on coatings alone for
  surface resistance (PDF p.69, sect. 6.3).
- MACHINE FORM: `virtual_drc.py CLEARANCE_MM = 0.2` is a single
  voltage-blind constant. Audit below. Candidate upgrade: a
  voltage-class-aware clearance check — per-net voltage band from the
  composition, B4 table for track-track, A6 for pad-pad.
- APPLICABILITY: all classes 1/2/3 and complexity levels A/B/C
  (PDF p.117, sect. 10.1.2); Table is functional insulation — NOT a
  safety-agency creepage standard.

### Audit vs PCBSmith (Class 2, <=100 V, mask-coated board + uncoated lands)

| Our constant | Value | IPC-2221B comparable | Verdict |
|---|---|---|---|
| `CLEARANCE_MM` (virtual_drc.py:36) | 0.2 | B4 0-30 V: 0.05; B4 31-100 V: 0.13 | **Tighter (safe)** for coated conductors at all our logic/power rails up to 100 V |
| `CLEARANCE_MM` as pad-to-pad (lands = uncoated, A6) | 0.2 | A6 0-15 V: 0.13; 16-30 V: 0.25; 31-50 V: 0.4; 51-100 V: 0.5 | **Tighter** <=15 V; **LOOSER** for nets >15 V peak: 24 V+ rails pad-to-foreign-copper wants 0.25-0.5 |
| `CLEARANCE_MM` if a board ships mask-less (B2) | 0.2 | B2 0-30 V: 0.1; 31-100 V: 0.64 | **LOOSER** above 30 V — never waive solder mask on >30 V boards without widening clearance |
| Flyback barrier gap (rules §10.1) | 6.4 mm | Table 6-1 171-250 V: B2 1.25 / B3 6.4 / B4 0.4 | Our 6.4 = the B3 (worst-case altitude, uncoated) cell — far beyond the sea-level table need; retained for reinforced-insulation intent, still capped needs_human_review |

Action items filed: (1) voltage-aware clearance for >15 V-peak nets
near uncoated pads (A6 column); (2) the `isolation_barrier` gap
justification should cite "Table 6-1 B3 171-250 V = 6.4 mm" rather
than "pollution-degree-2 tables" — IPC-2221B has no pollution-degree
table; pollution degrees are IEC 60950/62368 territory
(docs/pcb-design-rules.md §10.1 wording is imprecise, value fine).

## 2. Conductor width vs current

WHERE: PDF p.68, sect. 6.2; PDF p.114, sect. 10.1.1.

- **IPC-2221B contains no current/width chart.** Revision B defers:
  minimum width/thickness "should be in accordance with the universal
  charts provided in IPC-2152" (PDF p.68, sect. 6.2). The classic
  formula I = k * dT^0.44 * A^0.725 (A in sq. mil; k = 0.048
  external, k = 0.024 internal) is the curve fit to the **IPC-2221A
  Figure 6-4** nomographs, which revision B removed. It remains the
  industry shorthand and underestimates vs IPC-2152 for most
  single-conductor cases (conservative), but it is no longer printed
  in the B revision.
- Conductor thickness accounting: internal = base foil; external =
  foil + PTH plating, excluding solder/tin-lead/secondary platings;
  foil nominal varies +-10% (PDF p.68, sect. 6.2).
- Real finished thickness tables: internal 1 oz foil finishes at min
  24.9 um after processing (Table 10-1, PDF p.115); external 1 oz +
  Class 1/2 plating (20 um min) finishes at min 47.9 um; Class 3
  plating 25 um min -> 52.9 um (Table 10-2, PDF p.115).
- Representative points from our fitted formula at dT = 10 C, 1 oz
  (35 um) external: 0.3 mm -> ~1.0 A, 0.8 mm -> ~2.0 A (docstring
  hand-check), 2.0 mm -> ~3.9 A.
- Temperature rule: internal temperature rise from current + ambient
  shall not exceed laminate MOT (PDF p.37, sect. 4.x material note).
- MACHINE FORM: `calculators/electronics.py
  solve_trace_current_capacity` (k = 0.048, dT default 10 C, 35 um)
  feeding `design_checks.py _check_trace_currents` via
  `net_currents`. AUDIT: formula and k **match the legacy 2221A
  external fit exactly**; docstring/reference strings should say
  "IPC-2221A Fig. 6-4 fit; IPC-2221B defers to IPC-2152" for
  provenance honesty. Internal-layer runs (our dual-side boards are
  2-layer, both external, so k = 0.048 is correct today) would need
  k = 0.024 if we ever add inner layers. 10 C rise default is the
  conservative standard choice.
- APPLICABILITY: still air assumed; vacuum/altitude and adjacent-
  copper effects are why IPC-2152 exists — for >3 A or dense parallel
  high-current runs, do not trust the fit.

## 3. Annular ring, land size, and hole tolerances

WHERE: PDF p.107-113, sect. 9.1-9.2.

- **Minimum land diameter equation** (sect. 9.1.1, PDF p.107-108):
  `land_min = a + 2b + c` where a = max finished hole dia (external)
  or drill dia (internal); b = min annular ring (Table 9-2);
  c = fabrication allowance (Table 9-1).
- **Table 9-1 fabrication allowance c** (PDF p.108): Level A 0.4 mm,
  B 0.25 mm, C 0.2 mm. +50 um per extra oz of copper above 1 oz;
  +50 um for >8 layers.
- **Table 9-2 annular ring minimums** (PDF p.109, all classes):
  internal supported 25 um; external supported 50 um; external
  unsupported 0.150 mm. WHY (stated): unsupported value is larger to
  survive drilling without pad loss; brittle laminates benefit from
  oversize lands.
  - Worked Class 2 / Level B via example: 0.4 mm finished hole +
    2x0.05 + 0.25 -> **0.75 mm min external land** (0.375 mm radius).
    Our standard via: drill 0.4? No — `VIA_RADIUS_MM = 0.3` means
    0.6 mm land; that satisfies the equation only for finished holes
    <= 0.25 mm at Level B (0.25 + 0.1 + 0.25 = 0.6) or <= 0.3 mm at
    Level C. AUDIT: our 0.6/0.3 via geometry is a **Level C
    (reduced producibility)** design per Table 9-1 — legal, but
    should be stated in fab notes; a Level B fab wants 0.75 mm pads
    on 0.3 mm holes.
- Class 3 requires annular ring on all PTHs; Class 1/2 performance
  specs may allow partial breakout, but design should still avoid it
  (sect. 9.1.2, PDF p.108). Breakout-allowed designs shall use
  modified land shapes (filleting/keyhole/corner-entry, sect. 9.1,
  PDF p.107).
- **Hole location tolerance, Table 9-5** (PDF p.113): DTP Level A
  0.25 / B 0.2 / C 0.15 mm (drill positioning + drift only).
- **Min drilled hole, buried vias, Table 9-3** (PDF p.111): Class 2:
  0.10 mm (<0.25 mm layer), 0.15 mm (0.25-0.5 mm), 0.20 mm
  (>0.5 mm). **Blind vias, Table 9-4**: Class 2: 0.10 / 0.20 /
  0.30 mm by layer thickness bands. Microvia = blind, aspect <= 1:1,
  length <= 0.25 mm (PDF p.14, sect. 1.5.1).
- **Via protection, Table 9-6** (PDF p.114): filled/plugged hole dia
  0.254-1.27 mm bands; filled-via aspect ratio 3 min / 8 max; "no
  unprotected copper shall be allowed", single-sided protection with
  bare-copper barrel not allowed (sect. 9.3.1, PDF p.113).
- **Adjacent-hole spacing**: lands must meet 6.3 spacing AND residual
  laminate between holes >= 0.5 mm (sect. 9.2.7, PDF p.113).
- Pads on unsupported (NPTH) holes: not recommended; if used, add
  >= 0.38 mm radial clearance "donut" (sect. 9.2.1, PDF p.110).
- Nonfunctional internal lands: route with all lands in place, remove
  only after routing (sect. 10.1.3, PDF p.117).
- MACHINE FORM: `HOLE_EXTRA_MM = 0.05` in virtual_drc models the
  annulus we assume beyond drill — the real hole-to-copper foreign
  clearance is CLEARANCE + HOLE_EXTRA = 0.25 mm, consistent with the
  thermometer `~hole:` obstacle rule. The land-size equation is a
  NEW check candidate: `annular_ring` — for every PTH pad assert
  pad_radius >= drill/2 + ring + c/2 with declared producibility
  level. `min_through_hole` project constraint (0.2 mm drill floor)
  matches Class 2 buried-via floor for >0.5 mm cores — keep.
- APPLICABILITY: rigid boards; HDI/micro-BGA allowances live in
  IPC-2226 (Table 9-1 note 4); aspect ratio detail in IPC-2222.

## 4. Solder mask requirements

WHERE: PDF p.48-49, sect. 4.6.1; Table 4-16.

- Mask per IPC-SM-840; Class 3 boards need 840 Class H/FH
  (PDF p.48). No mask where board contacts guides.
- **Table 4-16 minimum clearances and dams** (PDF p.49):
  liquid screenable 0.25 / 0.25 mm; photoimageable dry film
  0.051 / 0.127 mm (thin) or 0.051 / 0.25 mm (0.066-0.1 mm film);
  **LPI 0.051 mm clearance, 0.1 mm dam** (our fabs are LPI).
- Mask over melting metal (HASL/tin-lead): complete coverage allowed
  only up to **1.3 mm conductor width**; wider needs relief windows
  >= 0.625 mm^2 on <= 6.35 mm grid; mask may overlap exposed melting
  metal by at most **1.0 mm** (PDF p.49). Not applicable to bare
  copper + LPI (our stack) but matters if HASL finish is chosen.
- Data files carry mask openings equal to lands; fabricator adjusts
  (PDF p.49). Unrelieved copper > 625 mm^2 -> adhesion promoter
  advisable (sect. 4.6.1.1).
- MACHINE FORM: KiCad default mask expansion (0.05-0.1 mm) already
  matches LPI 0.051 mm; a `mask_sliver` check (dam >= 0.1 mm between
  adjacent openings) is the encodable piece — fine-pitch parts
  (0.5 mm pitch, 0.3 mm pads -> 0.2 mm gap) go mask-defined or gang-
  opened, worth a WARNING finding.
- APPLICABILITY: mask type must be declared; values are "typical
  minimum" (table title), fab capability may override upward.

## 5. Silkscreen / marking

WHERE: PDF p.50, sect. 4.7.

- Marking by **nonconductive** inks/etch/labels; provide refdes,
  part/serial, revision, orientation/polarity, ESD status.
- Placement: avoid under components, hidden-after-assembly spots, and
  **conductive surfaces**; not on melting-metal surfaces; etched
  markings can change capacitance (PDF p.50).
- Conductive markings (if etched copper): may touch a conductor on
  one side but shall keep Table 6-1 spacing to adjacent conductors
  (sect. 10.1.2, PDF p.117).
- Orientation symbols: should be provided generally; **shall** for
  Class 3 (sect. 10.2.4, PDF p.118). Polarity marks for pin 1,
  cap +, diode cathode (sect. 8.1.3, PDF p.86).
- MACHINE FORM: our silk machinery (silk-over-pad DRC, 0.8 mm
  `MIN_SILK_TEXT_SIZE_MM`, refdes repositioning) already enforces the
  ink-off-copper intent; IPC gives no text height number — 0.8 mm is
  our fab-derived floor, unchallenged by the standard. Polarity-mark
  presence for polarized parts is a NEW design-check candidate
  (compositions know which roles are polarized).
- APPLICABILITY: all classes; symbol style free.

## 6. Component placement, spacing, orientation

WHERE: PDF p.85-88, sect. 8.1.

- **Component-to-board-edge >= 1.5 mm** (component edge = body edge,
  or land-pattern edge on leaded sides) for placement, soldering,
  test fixturing; else provide breakaway tabs (sect. 8.1.2,
  PDF p.86).
- **Lead/metal-case to any conductive path >= 0.13 mm** hard floor;
  uncoated conducting areas to hardware ~0.75 mm recommended, never
  below Table 6-1 (sect. 8.1.8 + Figure 8-4, PDF p.88).
- Worst-case component misregistration shall not reduce spacing to
  adjacent conductors below minimum electrical spacing (sect. 8.1.2,
  PDF p.85).
- Placement grid: 0.5 mm preferred (then 50 um); imperial 2.54 mm
  (then 1.27/0.64) — 2.54 mm eases bed-of-nails (PDF p.86).
- Orientation: polarized parts oriented consistently; components
  orthogonal to board edge, parallel/perpendicular to each other;
  stagger rows vs solder-wave travel; rectangular chips long-axis
  perpendicular to travel to avoid shadowing (sect. 8.1.2-8.1.3 +
  Figure 8-1, PDF p.86-87).
- Accessibility: every component removable without removing another;
  lands not obscured (sect. 8.1.4, PDF p.87) — throwaway assemblies
  exempt.
- Body extension: no part except connectors beyond board perimeter
  (sect. 8.1.5, PDF p.87). Axial bodies centered between holes
  (8.1.6). Parts < 5 g/lead and < 1 W: body in contact with board;
  >= 5 g/lead: clamps/adhesive, solder never the sole support
  (sect. 8.1.9, PDF p.88).
- MACHINE FORM: courtyard machinery covers part-to-part; NEW checks:
  (a) `component_edge_clearance` >= 1.5 mm body-to-outline (we only
  check copper today via EDGE_CLEARANCE_MM); (b) consistent-polarity
  orientation advisory (we have the LED-column direction check;
  generalize to diodes/electrolytics per board). Placement grid: our
  PLACEMENTS are already metric-snapped; keep 0.5 mm bias.
- APPLICABILITY: wave-solder orientation rules only for wave/selective
  processes; reflow-only boards may ignore shadowing.

## 7. Testpoint / testability rules

WHERE: PDF p.27-28, sect. 3.6.5 + Figures 3-2/3-3; PDF p.118,
sect. 10.2.3.

- Probe test-land diameter **>= 0.9 mm**; 0.6 mm acceptable on boards
  < 7700 mm^2 (PDF p.28 item a). All our boards to date are under
  7700 mm^2 -> 0.6 mm floor applies.
- Probe-site clearance: 80% of adjacent component height, min
  **0.6 mm**, max 5.0 mm (item b, Figure 3-2). Tall parts (> 5.7 mm
  on probe side): test lands >= 5.0 mm away (item c, Figure 3-3).
- **No parts or test lands within 3.0 mm of board edge** (item d).
- Test lands solder-coated/non-oxidizing, mask-free and marking-free
  (item e; also 10.2.3 "free of coating material prior to test").
- Probe lands/vias, never component leads or terminations — pressure
  masks opens/cold joints (item f, Figure 3-4). Single-side probing
  (bottom) preferred; bring nodes down with vias (item g).
- Test lands on 2.5 mm centers if possible (item h); distribute
  evenly to avoid board flex (item j); one test land per electrical
  node, named + located (item k); don't use gold edge fingers (i).
- Vias/wide conductors/component lands may serve as test points if
  probe area suffices (sect. 10.2.3, PDF p.118); identify on assembly
  drawing.
- MACHINE FORM: NEW check family if we ever emit testpoints:
  `testpoint_geometry` (dia >= 0.6/0.9 mm, mask opening present,
  0.6 mm keepout, 3.0 mm edge margin, one per net). Today PCBSmith
  emits none — a `coverage` advisory ("N nets lack probe access")
  would be the first honest step.
- APPLICABILITY: bed-of-nails/ICT manufacturing test; hobby/hand-
  probed boards need only the geometry floors.

## 8. Double-sided and mixed assembly

WHERE: PDF p.85, sect. 8.1.1.2/8.1.2; PDF p.92, sect. 8.2.3-8.2.4.

- Parts on both sides / THT+SMT mixing "requires complete
  understanding" of the assembly sequence (IPC-CM-770, IPC-SM-780);
  phase-1 parts must not collide with phase-2 insertion heads and
  phase-2 stresses must not hit phase-1 joints (8.1.1.2, 8.2.3).
- THT parts go on the side opposite the solder wave; axial/nonaxial
  leaded parts on one side only where possible (8.1.2, PDF p.85).
- Bottom-side SMT on wave-soldered boards: parts shall survive 260 C
  molten-solder immersion 5 s, ~120 C thermal shock at wave entry;
  wave-soldered chips need adhesive bonding (8.2.4, PDF p.92;
  8.1.2, PDF p.86).
- Component stacking prohibited in initial design (8.1.2, PDF p.85).
- Soldering survivability envelopes: wave 260 C/1 min, vapor phase
  216 C/4 min, other reflow 225 C/1 min; lead-free higher
  (8.2.4, PDF p.92).
- MACHINE FORM: our dual-side compaction (flyback back-side SMD)
  is reflow-both-sides — the encodable rule is an advisory: back-side
  parts on a wave-soldered board must be wave-rated + bonded; since
  our process assumption is reflow, record `assembly_process:
  "reflow"` in board metadata so the check has something to test.
- APPLICABILITY: process-dependent; wave rules only for THT-bearing
  boards (our servo/thermometer mix THT connectors + SMT).

## 9. Conductor routing and shape craft

WHERE: PDF p.117, sect. 10.1.3-10.1.4; PDF p.116 Figure 10-1.

- Preferred directions X, Y, 45 deg; conductor length minimized;
  direction changes with included angle < 90 deg should have corners
  rounded/chamfered (10.1.3). Matches our `trace_corner_angle`
  blocker (no acute joints) — IPC states it as "should", we enforce
  harder, fine.
- Neck-down permitted between obstacles if minimums hold at the neck
  (10.1.1/Figure 10-2, PDF p.117).
- Spacing should allow etch compensation, typically **2x copper
  thickness** (10.1.4, PDF p.117) — at 1 oz external (~48 um
  finished) that is ~0.1 mm inside the fab's allowance; our 0.2 mm
  grid clearance absorbs it.
- Min conductor width measured at conductor BASE (Figure 10-1,
  PDF p.116); production-master width compensation is fab's job, but
  finished-width tolerance defaults are 80-90% of supplied pattern
  (PDF p.115).
- UL-imposed minimum widths override (PDF p.115).
- MACHINE FORM: already enforced (route->smooth->merge->prune, corner
  check, redundant-copper check). The 2x-thickness etch compensation
  is the WHY behind never routing at exactly fab-minimum spacing.
- APPLICABILITY: all boards.

## 10. Miscellaneous normative values worth keeping

- Producibility levels A/B/C are communication, not requirements
  (PDF p.14, sect. 1.6.3) — but every table keyed on them requires us
  to DECLARE a level; PCBSmith geometry today is effectively Level C
  (smallest allowances) for lands, Level B for hole tolerance.
- Board classes: Class 1 general, 2 dedicated service, 3 high
  reliability (PDF p.14, sect. 1.6.2). User defines class in
  procurement docs — our bundles should state "Class 2" explicitly.
- Tooling hole tolerancing ~50 um, registration pins ~25 um
  (PDF p.112-113, sect. 9.2.5.1.1).
- Conformal coating thickness bands, e.g., AR/ER/UR 0.03-0.13 mm,
  SR 0.05-0.21 mm, XY 0.01-0.05 mm (Table 4-17, PDF p.50) — only if
  we ever declare A5/A7 clearances.
- Embedded capacitance: plane pairs <= 0.1 mm apart act as
  distributed bypass (PDF p.48, sect. 4.5.2).
- Metal-cased components insulated from adjacent conductive elements
  (PDF p.88, sect. 8.1.8/8.1.7 area); conductive areas under flush-
  mounted parts protected against moisture entrapment (8.1.7,
  PDF p.87).

## Top 10 most machine-encodable requirements (ranked)

1. **Table 6-1 voltage-banded clearance** (PDF p.69) — replace the
   single CLEARANCE_MM with per-net-voltage lookup: B4 for coated
   copper, A6 for pad-to-foreign-copper; today we are LOOSER than A6
   for any net > 15 V peak. Highest-value gap found in this audit.
2. **Land size = a + 2b + c annular-ring equation** (PDF p.107-108,
   Tables 9-1/9-2) — new `annular_ring` virtual check over every PTH
   pad + via, keyed on declared producibility level; flags our
   0.6 mm via pads as Level-C-only.
3. **Trace current fit provenance + internal k** (PDF p.68) — keep
   I = 0.048*dT^0.44*A^0.725 but relabel as IPC-2221A fit / 2152
   deferral; add k = 0.024 branch the day we route internal layers.
4. **Component body to board edge >= 1.5 mm** (PDF p.86) — new
   `component_edge_clearance` check (we only police copper edge
   clearance at 0.5 mm today; that constant is our fab rule, IPC has
   no direct copper-to-edge number — keep both).
5. **Residual laminate between adjacent holes >= 0.5 mm**
   (PDF p.113, sect. 9.2.7) — hole-edge-to-hole-edge check; cheap to
   add next to the existing `~hole:` obstacle machinery.
6. **Solder mask dam >= 0.1 mm / clearance >= 0.051 mm (LPI)**
   (Table 4-16, PDF p.49) — `mask_sliver` warning between adjacent
   pad openings on fine-pitch parts.
7. **Testpoint geometry floors**: land >= 0.6 mm (small boards),
   0.6 mm probe keepout, >= 3.0 mm from edge, mask-free, one node per
   land (PDF p.28) — whole family lands the day PCBSmith emits
   testpoints; until then a per-net probe-access advisory.
8. **Lead/metal-case 0.13 mm min + 0.75 mm hardware clearance**
   (PDF p.88) — mounting-hole/hardware keepout ring check around
   NPTH mounting holes (washer diameter vs copper).
9. **Consistent polarity orientation** (PDF p.86-87) — generalize the
   LED-column direction check: all polarized passives of a family
   share orientation where routing permits; advisory severity.
10. **Class/level declaration in the bundle** (PDF p.14) — emit
    "Class 2, producibility Level B/C per feature" into the review
    document so every table-keyed check has a declared context;
    zero geometry, pure honesty, enables 1-9.

## Extraction notes / limitations

- Current-carrying charts are genuinely absent from revision B
  (deferred to IPC-2152); the formula in our calculator is the
  2221A-era fit — flagged, not a bug in values, only in citation.
- Table 6-1 cell "B1 16-30 V" prints 0.05 mm in the cache text; some
  published 2221A copies show 0.1 — the B-revision cache clearly
  reads 0.05 for both 0-15 and 16-30 (PDF p.69 lines 35-62).
- Aspect-ratio limits for standard PTHs are in IPC-2222 (sectional),
  not here; only filled-via ratios (3-8) appear in Table 9-6.
- No pollution-degree or CTI-banded creepage exists in this standard;
  safety creepage claims must cite IEC 60950-1/62368-1 instead.
