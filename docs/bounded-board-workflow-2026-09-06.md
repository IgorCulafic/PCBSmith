## Operating contract

1. **Budget before preparation.** Classify using electrical novelty, qualification needs, density, interfaces and mechanics, not part count alone. Initial defaults:

   | Work class | Working target | Total stop limit |
   | --- | --- | --- |
   | Simple, familiar two-layer circuit such as the four-LED sequencer | 20 minutes | 30 minutes |
   | Moderate circuit/integration or constrained placement | 45 minutes | 60 minutes |
   | Complex or unfamiliar design | Scoped estimate before work | Explicit finite first work package; default no more than 120 minutes |

   Complex projects can have declared work packages. Do not split or reclassify a simple board after failure to evade its cap. A deadline authorizes stopping, not accepting an unchecked design. Refine defaults transparently using measurements, never silently during a job.

2. **Count the whole job.** Keep one persistent job ID and ledger outside replaceable attempt directories. Include requirements review, research, assets, vector planning, native work, engineering/visual review, corrections and handover. Record total wall time and identifiable tool time separately; unknown attribution stays unknown. Only explicit waiting for required user input may pause the operational allowance; total elapsed time must still be reported. Retries, profile changes, cancellation/resume and context recovery retain the deadline and counters. Extensions require an explicit user decision and retained old/new limits.

3. **Reserve verification.** Reserve at least 20% of the allowance for applicable native checks, final inspection and concise handover. At reserve entry, finish the best candidate or stop with its exact status; start no optional improvements. Cancellation, child-process termination and checkpoint/report cleanup get a separately bounded grace period, initially 30 seconds. Report any overrun rather than hiding cleanup time.

4. **Bound corrections across the job.** Allow one initial candidate plus at most two corrective cycles. A cycle batches diagnosed necessary edits and affected checks; individual edits in that batch are not separate redesigns. Routing/placement strategy retries consume the same allowance, with no more than three executions of either stage. Record the failure signature, changed inputs/settings and predicted benefit. Allow at most one materially different strategy escalation within the existing deadline. Reject identical retries without effective changes; recurrence of the same failure after its targeted correction stops that line of work. Rebuilds require the existing explicit decision and consume the same allowance.

5. **Stop at sufficient quality.** Fix violations of the brief, electrical/geometry checks, readability, assembly or evidence requirements. Once those criteria pass, record optional appearance/optimization ideas for later. Unresolved mandatory findings produce a blocked/unverified candidate, never a fabricated pass. Missing shared capabilities become separate platform work; do not develop new orchestration machinery inside a simple board run.

6. **Make vector planning explicit and lightweight.** Before native PCB placement, create or reuse an editable, dimensioned floorplan with outline, required holes/interfaces, component bodies/courtyards and orientation, functional groups, critical route/return corridors and key labels. Mark provisional geometry. Use the same structured coordinates/constraints for vector and native placement; inspect the SVG, present its preview and retain the review/anchor comparison. Local revisions update only affected intent; unchanged approved geometry is reusable. Final copper exports cannot substitute for this stage. SVGs do not establish electrical correctness. No image generation or repeated user approval is required unless requested or a genuine scope decision arises.

7. **Render according to invalidation.** Use vector/2D views during geometry iteration. Produce required expensive 3D views when geometry/model inputs stabilize; rerun only invalidated views through supported exact-identity checks. Respect the current review profile's evidence requirements. Model changes invalidate relevant 3D evidence; geometry changes invalidate affected views/checks. Explain every complete rerender. Never skip required checks or reuse stale evidence to meet the clock.

## Diagnostic checkpoint after failed corrections (agreed 2026-09-08)

When another correction would exceed the allowance, an operation exhausts its attempts, the same recorded failure recurs, or the final corrective cycle fails, queue a broader diagnostic checkpoint. The assistant should also trigger it early when inspection shows no meaningful progress; software does not infer semantic stagnation from arbitrary logs.

Before assessment use: pcbsmith board-job diagnose-start ROOT --reason "..."
The single window is at most five minutes and ends before the original verification reserve. It includes reading evidence and writing the recommendation. It cannot restart in another chat or after cancellation. If no time remains, stop; a retrospective review requires separately scoped analysis and must not reopen the old job.

Submit: pcbsmith board-job diagnose-complete ROOT --assessment ASSESSMENT.json
The DiagnosticRecommendation schema in board_job.py requires cause, progress_assessment, root_cause_analysis, alternatives_considered, one action (local_edit/rebuild/platform_work/stop), rationale, estimated_seconds, verification_criteria, uncertainties and evidence (job-relative paths to exact SHA-256 hashes). Inspect the actual evidence; an assertion is not proof of engineering correctness.

A pending, expired or completed checkpoint blocks new build/correction work. Verification remains available inside the original job deadline. Completing diagnosis never resets attempts, counters, time or authorizes the recommended action. This implementation adds no budget-extension or diagnostic-release override: further work needs an explicit allowance decision and a supported continuation mechanism, not manual ledger editing or a replacement job root.

The runtime validates the window and evidence at command boundaries and during evidence hashing. It does not forcibly interrupt assistant reasoning or arbitrary external tools. Diagnosis is a bounded decision point, not another unbounded design/research phase.
